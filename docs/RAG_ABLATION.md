# RAG ablation

Generated by `make rag-eval`. Configurations are ranked on the dev split only; the test split is reported for the chosen one.

Questions: 70 dev, 30 test. Chunks: fixed: 96, section: 104, document: 50.

How to read the numbers:

- Passage metrics (R@k, MRR) count a hit only if the chunk holds the gold evidence sentence. They favor coarse chunks, because a whole-policy chunk always contains it.
- Doc metrics count a hit when the chunk comes from the gold policy version. They do not depend on chunk size, so they are used for selection.
- Stale@5 is the share of the top five that is superseded, expired, or not yet in force.
- One question is worth 1.4 points on dev and 3.3 on test; smaller gaps are noise.
- Selection: keep configurations within one dev question of the best doc R@3 and doc MRR, then take the one with fewer stale hits, shorter context, smaller model, and simpler retrieval mode, in that order.
