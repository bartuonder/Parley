## Disputed invoices

When a customer has disputed an invoice with their bank, the invoice shows the disputed status. No refund can be issued for it, because a refund would pay the customer twice.

## What to do

Explain that the dispute is being handled by the bank and our disputes team. Do not promise an outcome. Escalate to a human if the customer wants to withdraw the dispute.
