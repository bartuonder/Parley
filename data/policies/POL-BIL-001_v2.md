## Rule

Two paid invoices for the same subscription with the same amount, issued within 24 hours of each other, are a duplicate charge. The later invoice is refunded in full without a waiting period. This replaces the 1-hour rule and the investigation period of version 1.

## Which charge

Only the later invoice is refundable. The earlier invoice is the legitimate charge and stays as it is.

## Evidence

Confirm the duplicate on the invoice list before refunding. A customer's claim alone is not enough.
