## Return window

Physical items can be returned for a refund within 30 days of delivery. This replaces the 14-day window of version 1.

## Condition

Items must be unused and in their original packaging. The window is counted from the delivery date shown on the carrier record, not from the order date.

## Exclusions

Final-sale items and digital goods follow their own policies and are not covered by this window.
