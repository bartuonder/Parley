## Duties and taxes

Customs duties and import taxes are the customer's responsibility and are not refunded when an order is returned.

## Refused or undeliverable parcels

A parcel that the carrier returns to the sender is treated as a lost shipment and refunded in full under the lost shipments policy.

## Estimates

International delivery estimates are listed in the standard delivery times policy.
