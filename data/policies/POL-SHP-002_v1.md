## Status meanings

- Label created: the carrier has a label but has not collected the parcel yet.
- In transit: the carrier is moving the parcel.
- Delivered: the carrier confirmed delivery.
- Lost: the carrier declared the parcel lost.
- Returned: the parcel went back to the sender.

## Using tracking numbers

A customer can be given the carrier name, tracking number, and current status. Do not guess a delivery date beyond the estimate on record.
