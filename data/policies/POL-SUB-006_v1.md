## Reminders

Customers on annual plans receive an email reminder 7 days before renewal.

## Unexpected renewal complaints

An annual renewal can be refunded in full if the customer asks within 14 days of the charge. After that the charge is not refundable, and the customer can cancel to avoid the next renewal. Monthly renewals are not refundable for this reason.
