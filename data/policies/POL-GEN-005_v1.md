## Rule

The policy in force on the date of the customer's request applies, not the policy in force when the order was placed. A policy with a future effective date does not apply yet, and a promotion that has expired does not apply.

## Conflicts

When two policies appear to disagree, prefer the one that is currently in force. If one is more specific, such as the premium member policy, prefer the specific one for the customers it covers.
