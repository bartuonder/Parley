## Always escalate

- The customer asks for something the agent has no tool for, such as pausing a subscription, cancelling an unshipped order, or changing company details on an invoice.
- The case involves a disputed invoice that the customer wants withdrawn.
- The customer asks for a refund the policy does not allow and keeps insisting.
- The refund exceeds the approval threshold. The refund is created for approval, and the customer is told it is under review.

## Handover

State the reason in a short note so the human does not need to ask the customer again. Tell the customer a human will follow up.
