## Rule

An annual charge can be refunded in full within 14 days of the charge date. This replaces the 30-day period of version 1.

## Scope

The cooling-off period covers both the first charge and renewals. It does not apply to monthly plans. After 14 days an annual charge is not refundable, although the subscription can still be cancelled for the next renewal.
