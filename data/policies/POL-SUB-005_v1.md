## Retries

When a renewal payment fails, the subscription becomes past due and the payment is retried over the following 7 days. Access continues during that time.

## Customer actions

The customer should update their payment method. A failed payment is not a charge, so there is nothing to refund for it.

## End of retries

If every retry fails, the subscription is cancelled automatically.
