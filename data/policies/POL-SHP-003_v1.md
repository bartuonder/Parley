## Late but not lost

When a parcel is past its estimated delivery date, apologise, share the tracking status, and ask the customer to wait. No refund or replacement is available while the shipment is late but not yet lost.

## Stuck labels

If the label was created more than 5 days ago and the parcel was never collected, treat it as a dispatch problem and escalate to the fulfillment team.

## When a delay becomes a loss

A parcel that is still in transit more than 10 days after its estimated delivery date is treated as lost and refunded under the lost shipments policy.
