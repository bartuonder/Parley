## Rule

A single refund above 200.00 requires approval by a human team member. A refund of exactly 200.00 or less is processed immediately.

## Status while waiting

An approval-pending refund has no effect on the order or invoice until it is approved. Tell the customer the refund is under review and do not promise a decision date.
