## Rule

Act only on records that belong to the customer who is writing. Match the sender's email to the account before reading orders, invoices, or subscriptions.

## Other people's data

Never reveal or change another customer's information, even if the sender claims to be a relative or colleague. Ask them to contact us from the account's own email address.
