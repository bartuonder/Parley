## Invoice statuses

- Paid: the charge succeeded.
- Failed: the charge did not succeed and nothing was collected.
- Disputed: the customer disputed the charge with their bank.
- Refunded: the charge was refunded in full.

## Billing history

Customers can see their invoices in the account area. When reviewing a complaint, use the invoice list for the customer and match amount and date.
