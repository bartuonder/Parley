## Rule

A subscription can be cancelled at any time. Cancellation is scheduled for the end of the current billing period, and the customer keeps access until then. This replaces the immediate, pro-rata cancellation of version 1.

## No partial-period refunds

Monthly charges are not refunded for the unused part of a period. The exceptions are duplicate charges and the annual cooling-off period.

## Repeat requests

Cancelling a subscription that is already scheduled to cancel changes nothing. A subscription that has already ended cannot be cancelled again.
