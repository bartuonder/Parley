## Rule

A shipment is treated as lost when the carrier marks it lost, when it was returned to the sender, or when it is still in transit more than 10 days after its estimated delivery date.

## Remedy

Lost shipments are refunded in full. The standard return window does not apply, and final-sale items on the order are included.

## Before the 10 days

If a shipment is late but not yet lost, share the tracking status and the estimated date. A refund is not available until the shipment qualifies as lost.
