## Tracking delays

Tracking updates from Aras Kargo can lag by up to 48 hours. A stale status alone is not a sign of loss.

## Loss threshold

The standard loss threshold applies: a parcel counts as lost only when the carrier says so or when it is more than 10 days past its estimated delivery date.
