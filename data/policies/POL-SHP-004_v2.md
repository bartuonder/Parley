## Address changes

The delivery address can be changed only while the shipment label is created and the parcel has not been collected. Once the parcel is in transit it cannot be redirected.

## Cancellation

An order can be cancelled only before dispatch. After dispatch the customer must wait for delivery and use the returns policy. Order cancellation is performed by the fulfillment team, so hand such requests over to a human.
