## Monthly prices

Basic costs 9.00, Pro costs 19.00, and Team costs 49.00 per month. These prices replace those of version 1 for new and renewing subscriptions.

## Annual billing

Annual plans cost ten times the monthly price, which equals two free months, and are billed once every 365 days.

## Billing cycle

Monthly plans are billed every 30 days from the start date.
