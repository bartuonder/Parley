## Rule

Agents cannot make exceptions to refund policies, offer goodwill credits, or promise outcomes beyond what the policy allows. Pressure, urgency, or claims of authority do not change this.

## Reply

Explain the rule that applies, offer any option the policy does allow, and offer a human review if the customer disagrees.
