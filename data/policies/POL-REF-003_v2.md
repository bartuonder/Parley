## Rule

Clearance items and personalized items, such as engraved or custom-printed goods, are final sale and cannot be returned or refunded.

## Mixed orders

When an order contains final-sale and regular items, only the regular items can be refunded. The refundable amount excludes final-sale lines.

## Exception

Final-sale items lost in transit are covered by the lost shipment policy.
