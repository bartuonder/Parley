## Estimates

Domestic orders (United States and Canada) arrive in 2 to 5 days after dispatch. International orders arrive in 5 to 12 days after dispatch.

## Dispatch

Orders are dispatched within 1 to 3 days of being placed. Delivery estimates start at dispatch, not at order time.

## Estimated delivery date

The estimated delivery date on the shipment record is the date quoted to the customer. It is an estimate, not a guarantee.
