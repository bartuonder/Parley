## Refundable amount

A refund covers the price of the eligible items. The refundable maximum is the eligible item total minus any refunds already processed or waiting for approval.

## Partial refunds

A customer can ask for a refund of some items only. Several partial refunds are allowed on one order as long as their sum stays within the refundable maximum.

## Pending refunds

A refund waiting for approval counts against the refundable maximum until it is rejected.
